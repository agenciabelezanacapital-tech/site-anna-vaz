Looking for the best balayage salon near Boston? What to check before you book

How to choose a balayage and blonde specialist north of Boston (Malden, Medford, Everett, Melrose, Somerville, Revere), and why clients drive to Anna Vaz Concept for their blonde.

Anna Vaz Concept · Malden, MA · 2026-10-06

Type “best balayage near me” around Boston and you get hundreds of salons. Most of them do balayage. Very few specialize in it. If you are north of the city, in Malden, Medford, Everett, Melrose, Revere, Somerville or Saugus, here is how to pick a colorist you will keep for years.

Look for a specialist, not a menu

A salon that lists balayage next to pedicures and eyelash extensions is not necessarily bad, but a colorist whose entire portfolio is blondes sees your type of hair every day. Check the Instagram: is it full of consistent, healthy blondes on different starting colors, or a mix of everything?

Read the reviews for the word “blonde”

Five stars for a haircut tell you nothing about color. Search the Google reviews for blonde, balayage, highlights, color. At Anna Vaz Concept, reviews say things like “she's the only person I trust with my blonde” and “she pulled the blonde from my roots in a way nobody had managed before.” That is the kind of signal you want.

Ask how they protect the hair

A real blonde specialist will talk about bond-builders, staged lifting and formaldehyde-free treatments before you ask. If the answer to “can I go platinum today from dark brown” is a cheerful yes with no questions, keep looking.

Expect a consultation before a price

Flat online prices for balayage are a red flag for complex work. The right colorist wants to see your hair first. Anna Vaz does her consultations on WhatsApp with photos, then sends a plan and a quote.
