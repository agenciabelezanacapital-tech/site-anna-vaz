Questions

Before you go blonde

How much does balayage cost at Anna Vaz Concept?

It depends on your starting color, length, density and the result you want, so Anna quotes after a quick WhatsApp consultation with photos. In Greater Boston, balayage and blonding services generally range from a few hundred dollars for a partial to more for full blonding or color correction. Your quote includes the treatment and toner.

How long does a balayage or blonding session take?

Plan 3 to 5 hours for balayage or highlights, and longer for a full blonding session or color correction. Anna tells you the estimated time when she confirms your appointment.

Can I go blonde from dark or previously colored hair?

Usually yes, in stages. Dark or box-dyed hair often needs two or more sessions to reach a clean blonde without damage. Anna plans the path and tells you honestly what is possible on day one.

Balayage, highlights or babylights: what is the difference?

Highlights use foils for a brighter, more uniform blonde. Babylights are ultra-fine highlights for a natural look. Balayage is hand-painted for soft, sun-kissed dimension that grows out without a line. Bronde is balayage in caramel and honey tones on brunette hair.

How often do I need to come back?

Lived-in balayage can go 3 to 5 months. Highlights and babylights usually 8 to 12 weeks. A gloss or toner visit every 6 to 8 weeks keeps the blonde bright.

Will my hair get damaged?

Protecting the hair is the point of the method: bond-builders during lightening, formaldehyde-free treatments and Kérastase care. Anna will not push your hair past what it can take in one session.
